INTRODUCTION

Most organizations treat marketing as promotion: campaigns, channels and activity metrics, brought in after the important decisions are already made. Then they wonder why growth depends on discounting, why good products fail to be chosen, and why strategy keeps drifting away from what customers actually value.

Marketing Reclassified argues that the problem is not poor marketing. It is misplaced marketing.

The book puts marketing back where it belongs: upstream, as the system that brings market reality into the decisions that shape what an organization offers, prices, promises and scales.

AUDIENCE

Who Is This Book For?

  • Board members, CEOs, business owners, CMOs, and CFOs who want marketing to improve decisions, not only produce activity.
  • Strategy, product, sales, and customer-experience leaders who influence organizational value creation.
  • Entrepreneurs who want to build marketing deliberately from the beginning.
  • Marketing professionals and consultants who advise organizational leadership.

The book is relevant to anyone whose decisions influence what an organization offers, whom it serves, how it creates value, or how it grows.

What This Book Is Not

Marketing Reclassified is not a conventional marketing textbook or a collection of promotional tactics.

It does not discard established marketing knowledge.

Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning (STP), the 4Ps, and the promotion mix remain relevant. They are reframed as evidence-backed governance decisions within one integrated system.

The objective is not to replace marketing’s existing tools, but to reconsider the organizational purpose those tools are expected to serve.
